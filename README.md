# Kenecee Foods — Food Business Website

Kenecee Foods is a responsive food business website designed to showcase a local restaurant and snack business, its menu, products, gallery, and contact information.

The project focuses on creating a clean, mobile-friendly business website with strong visual presentation and clear calls to action.

> **Project status:** Frontend demo website. No backend, database, authentication, or online ordering system is currently connected.

## Tech Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Responsive Web Design

## Features

* Responsive navigation bar
* Hero section with business introduction
* Call Now CTA
* Menu navigation
* About section
* Food and snack menu showcase
* Food gallery
* Contact information
* Click-to-call phone link
* Contact form interface
* Mobile-responsive layout
* Food-focused visual presentation

## Menu Showcase

The website presents several food categories including:

* Meat Pie
* Jumbo Roll
* Sausage Roll
* Cakes & Desserts

## Business Information

The demo presents Kenecee Foods as a local food business based in **Awkuzu, Anambra State**, with business address, phone contact, and opening-hours information.
